Junction · earth desk

The planet is a materials problem wearing a climate costume.

jEarth is two rooms in one house. The first is a waste guidebook: how to check a bag, what wet and dry actually mean, how to dispose of each stream, and which countries already run the system. The second is a mushroom farm: cameras on the crop, honest harvest weight, booking through junctionBack.
